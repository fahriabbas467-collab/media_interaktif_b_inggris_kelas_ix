Material: Back to the 90s

Life in the 1990s was very different from today. People did not have smartphones, high-speed internet, or social media. Let's learn some words about things people used back then!

A. Pop Culture & Things in the 90s

Walkman

A portable music player. People used it to listen to music everywhere.

Cassette Tape

Before MP3s and Spotify, music was recorded on these magnetic tapes.

Tamagotchi

A popular digital pet toy that kids had to feed and take care of.

Payphone

Public phones on the street that required coins to make a call.

Film Camera

Cameras used physical film rolls. You had to wait days to see the photos!

CRT Television

Big, heavy, box-shaped TVs before flat screens were invented.

B. Grammar: Simple Past Tense

We use the Simple Past Tense to talk about actions or events that happened and finished in the past (e.g., yesterday, in 1995, last week).

Positive (+)

Subject + Verb 2 + Object

  • I played Tamagotchi every day. (Regular: +ed)
  • My father bought a Walkman in 1998. (Irregular)

Negative (-)

Subject + did not + Verb 1

  • They did not have smartphones.
  • She didn't listen to MP3s.

*Note: Verb changes back to V1 after "did not".

Interrogative (?)

Did + Subject + Verb 1 ?

  • Did you watch TV yesterday?
  • Did he call from a payphone?
To Be in the Past: Use Was (for I, He, She, It) and Were (for You, We, They).
Example: The movie was great. We were happy.
